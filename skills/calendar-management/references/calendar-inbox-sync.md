# Calendar Inbox sync

Represent every relevant timed commitment on its exact dated `Inbox` as a toggle whose title begins with local start and end time. This includes meetings, appointments, travel, workouts, focus blocks, and physical events.

Link the title to the best canonical source. Use toggle children only for lightweight preparation and supporting documents. Do not duplicate full meeting notes.

- Event moved: update its time and chronological position.
- Event canceled or declined: remove its active toggle.
- Overlap: keep both events, mention the conflict in Brief, and recommend a resolution without changing either event automatically.
- Passive all-day reminder: use an ordinary bullet on the exact date and never carry it.
- Acknowledged reminder: remove the Inbox projection and preserve the calendar event as history.
- Canceled or moved reminder: update the calendar first, then the projection.

Do not create future Inbox pages merely because calendar events exist.
