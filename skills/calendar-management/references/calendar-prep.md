Load before building a prep brief.
## When to prep
Every status or day run checks meetings in the next 24 hours. Prepare context for meetings that are external, VIP, unclear, first-time, or likely to need decisions.
Tentative or declined events get no prep unless the user asks.
## Brief contents
Include only what helps the user walk in ready.
- Why the meeting exists.
- Who is attending and why they matter.
- Relevant project, client, contact, task, thread, page, or document.
- Recent messages or updates.
- Prior decisions or promises.
- Open questions.
- Suggested agenda or talking points.
- Whether the user is actually needed.
## Persistence
Check prior Inbox entries, email threads, pages, and project context before building the brief. Recurring meetings should reference last discussion, promised follow-ups, and unresolved items.
After the meeting, convert promised follow-up into To do. Remove prep when the meeting passes and no follow-up remains.
