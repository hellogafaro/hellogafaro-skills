## Acceptance checks
Every meeting must pass these before accepting or scheduling.
1. Clear purpose. One sentence outcome exists.
2. User needed. Attendance is required for decision, relationship, or accountability.
3. Not better async. A doc, email, or message would not do the same job.
If any answer is no, raise it before acting. Propose async, shorter format, delegate, or decline.

## Recurring review
On deep runs, flag recurring meetings with no clear agenda, weak attendance need, repeated skips, or obvious async replacement. Propose cancellation or format change. Do not cancel without confirmation.
