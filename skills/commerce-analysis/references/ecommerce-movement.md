# ecommerce movement diagnostics

Use this when Shopify or store performance moved and the reason is unclear.

## Revenue equation

Revenue movement usually comes from:

- Orders
- AOV
- Refunds
- Discounts
- Product mix
- Customer mix
- Availability

Do not stop at revenue. Decompose enough to find the driver.

## Product mix

Check whether movement is concentrated in:

- One product or variant
- One collection
- A bundle
- Discounted items
- New products
- Out-of-stock products
- High-return products

## Customer mix

Separate when useful:

- New customers
- Returning customers
- First-time buyers
- Repeat buyers
- High-LTV cohorts
- Geo or market

## Promo and margin

Discount-driven revenue can hide margin pressure. When available, inspect:

- Gross sales
- Net sales
- Discount amount
- Refund amount
- Shipping and tax treatment
- Gross margin or COGS

## Output

Name the movement type:

- Demand
- Conversion
- AOV
- Product mix
- Customer mix
- Promo
- Refunds
- Inventory
- Tracking
