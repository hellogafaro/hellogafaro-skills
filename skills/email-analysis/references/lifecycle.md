# lifecycle diagnostics

Use this when email or SMS performance needs more than a quick metric read.

## Campaign checks

- Revenue per recipient separates list size from message quality.
- Click rate and conversion rate are better action signals than open rate.
- Unsubscribe and spam complaint rate can turn a revenue win into a list-health problem.
- Compare promo campaigns against similar promo campaigns, not only against non-promo sends.
- Segment by buyers, non-buyers, recent purchasers, engaged subscribers, and VIPs when useful.

## Flow checks

- Welcome: list capture quality, first purchase conversion, time to first purchase.
- Abandoned cart: checkout friction, discount strategy, timing, product mix, recovery rate.
- Browse abandonment: intent quality, product availability, category interest.
- Post-purchase: second purchase, cross-sell, review request, support friction.
- Winback: reactivation rate, discount dependency, unsubscribe risk.

## Deliverability flags

- Bounce, spam, unsubscribe, and suppressed profiles rising together suggest list-quality risk.
- Open rate drops alone can be privacy or inbox placement. Confirm with clicks and revenue.
- Revenue rising while engagement falls can signal over-mailing or promo dependency.

## Output

Separate:

- List size effect
- Segment quality effect
- Message or offer effect
- Deliverability effect
- Attribution or tracking effect
