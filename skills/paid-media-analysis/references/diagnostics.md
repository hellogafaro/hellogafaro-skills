# paid media diagnostics

Use this when a paid channel moved and the cause is not obvious.

## Common patterns

- Spend up, revenue flat: check CVR, audience quality, campaign mix, attribution lag, and Shopify revenue.
- CPA up, CTR flat: post-click issue, conversion tracking change, product/offer issue, or checkout issue.
- CPA up, CTR down: creative fatigue, audience mismatch, placement mix, or competitive pressure.
- CPM up, CTR flat: auction pressure, audience too narrow, seasonality, or budget shock.
- ROAS up, revenue down: spend was cut, budget shifted to efficient but smaller pockets, or attribution changed.
- Platform ROAS up, Shopify revenue flat: attribution inflation, duplicated conversions, delayed revenue, or returning customer credit.
- Frequency up, CTR down: likely fatigue, but confirm creative age and audience size.
- Conversion volume down, CVR stable: traffic volume issue.
- Conversion volume down, CVR down: funnel, offer, tracking, product, or audience issue.

## Budget recommendation checks

Before recommending more spend:

- Tracking is trustworthy enough.
- Campaign has enough conversion volume for the bid strategy.
- Marginal CPA or ROAS is acceptable, not just average CPA or ROAS.
- Inventory, landing page, and promo context do not block scale.
- The recommendation names what to scale and what not to scale.

Before recommending a cut:

- The issue is not a short attribution lag.
- Promo, stockout, tracking, or landing page events did not create a temporary distortion.
- The cut does not remove useful prospecting volume needed for retargeting.

## Output

State the driver category:

- Volume
- Price
- Quality
- Mix
- Fatigue
- Tracking
- External context
