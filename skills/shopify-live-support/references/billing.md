# Shopify billing support

## Scope and ownership

Use this reference for Shopify invoices, plans, subscriptions, app charges, billing credits, duplicate or unexpected charges, proration, and billing ownership. Start from **Settings > Billing** or the relevant app billing record.

Identify whether Shopify, an app developer, a payment provider, or a card issuer owns the disputed charge. Use the dedicated `shopify-chargeback` skill for card-network disputes. Do not describe a charge as unauthorized, refundable, or erroneous until the record or advisor confirms it.

## Evidence

Collect only what supports the issue:

- store and billing account;
- invoice, charge, transaction, subscription, plan, or app name;
- amount, currency, charge date, and billing period;
- expected price or treatment and the actual result;
- prior credits, refunds, cancellations, or advisor commitments;
- screenshots or invoice lines without full card details.

## Work toward the outcome

1. Ask the advisor to identify the exact charge and its owner.
2. Ask for the itemized calculation, policy, or billing event that produced it.
3. Request the specific correction, explanation, credit review, refund review, or owner handoff.
4. If escalation is required, confirm the billing issue, amount, requested action, and deadline are in the escalation.
5. Obtain the case reference and continuation channel.

Any credit, refund, charge, plan change, cancellation, settlement, or other money-related action requires explicit user approval before acceptance or submission. The agent may investigate eligibility and request written options without approval.

## Completion

Record the verified charge or invoice, owner, decision, amount, effective date, approval requirement, case reference, deadline, and next action. Distinguish a requested review from an approved credit or refund.
