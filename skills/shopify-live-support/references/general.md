# General Shopify support

## Scope and ownership

Use this reference for storefront, checkout, orders not primarily involving carrier delivery, fulfillment behavior, inventory, products, customers, reports, apps, integrations, themes, domains, markets, taxes, account access, Shopify Admin defects, and other issues outside billing or shipping.

Identify whether Shopify, an app vendor, a domain provider, a payment gateway, or another platform owns the failing surface. Use Shopify live support for Shopify-owned behavior, ownership clarification, account-specific access, platform defects, and documented escalation.

## Evidence

Collect only what supports the issue:

- affected store, organization, market, role, and timezone;
- affected URL, feature, order, resource, app, theme, or domain;
- exact expected and actual behavior;
- timestamps and reproducible steps;
- browser, device, error text, and request identifier when relevant;
- screenshots or a short recording;
- business or customer impact;
- prior cases, workarounds, and advisor commitments.

Never transmit passwords, MFA codes, recovery codes, API secrets, or unnecessary customer data. The user must complete unavoidable MFA, recovery, identity, or account-owner verification.

## Work toward the outcome

1. Reproduce or confirm the current state when safe and relevant.
2. Ask the advisor to check known incidents, account configuration, logs, or the affected record.
3. Request the specific fix, workaround, ownership decision, or escalation.
4. For third-party ownership, obtain the exact external owner and the evidence needed for a clean handoff.
5. For Shopify escalation, confirm the issue, reproduction, impact, requested action, owner, case reference, and continuation channel.

## Completion

Record the verified behavior, owner, fix or workaround, case reference, continuation channel, deadline, and next action. Distinguish a reproduced defect or filed escalation from a verified fix.
