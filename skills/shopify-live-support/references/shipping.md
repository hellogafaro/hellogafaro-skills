# Shopify shipping support

## Scope and ownership

Use this reference for Shopify Shipping labels, carrier delays, lost or damaged packages, delivery disputes, insurance claims, carrier tracers, investigation documents, claim determinations, and Guaranteed Service Refund requests.

Confirm who sold the label. When the label was bought through Shopify Shipping, Shopify Support or Shopify Shipping owns the carrier-side workflow and should contact the carrier on the merchant's behalf. Use the carrier directly only when the merchant bought the label directly or Shopify explicitly documents an external handoff.

## Evidence

Collect only what supports the active shipment:

- store, order, fulfillment, label, and tracking identifiers;
- carrier and service level;
- label purchase source;
- destination and shipment date when relevant;
- promised delivery window and scan history;
- current classification: delayed, lost, damaged, misdelivered, or not delivered;
- insurance or carrier claim identifier;
- requested document or remedy;
- prior Shopify cases, commitments, and continuation channels.

Keep similar shipments separate. State the active order clearly, correct unrelated-order confusion immediately, and do not reopen a closed case. Use old cases only as supporting history.

## Work toward the outcome

1. Check whether the existing case already contains the carrier document, determination, refund eligibility, or requested result.
2. Ask Shopify to perform the carrier-side action now: trace, investigation, document retrieval, claim, or service-refund review.
3. If the advisor lacks access, request Shopify Shipping or the carrier-support team with the required authority.
4. Confirm the escalation explicitly names the active order, tracking number, claim, shipment classification, and requested document or action.
5. Obtain the owner, case reference, continuation channel, and response expectation.

Do not accept a generic "the right team will review it" as confirmation that a tracer, claim document, or carrier request was included. Ask directly what the escalation instructs the team to obtain or do.

## Completion

Report the result precisely:

- document obtained;
- carrier request submitted;
- Shopify Shipping escalation submitted;
- escalation promised but not yet created;
- case reference pending;
- follow-up email promised;
- no timeframe available.

Never describe an escalation as if the package investigation, insurance claim, or refund is complete.
